Is my calendar private?

Yes. The app reads only the calendars you pick, right on your iPhone, and never uploads them. There are no accounts and no tracking. Events that look private (doctor visits, money, work and similar) are left out by default. You can add your own “never show” words in Settings, or press and hold any event in the paper to hide it. To turn calendar access off, go to iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I print?

Tap Print today's paper on the home screen and choose your AirPrint printer. Letter-size paper works best. To print from a computer or save the paper, tap the share button and send the PDF to Files, Mail or Messages. You can reprint any day from Past papers. Printing and sharing need an active subscription or free trial.

Printer not showing up? Make sure your iPhone and printer are on the same Wi-Fi network, and that the printer supports AirPrint.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Family Paper, and choose Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge. You can keep using it until the end of the period you've paid for. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page. Apple handles all payments.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the app, tap Print (or Settings › See plans), and tap Restore purchases. Your subscription unlocks again. Past papers are stored only on the original phone and don't move to a new device.

Weather says “unavailable”

Check that your ZIP code in Settings is correct and that you're online, then pull down on the home screen to refresh.
