Privacy Policy

Family Paper: Daily Printable (“the app”) is made by Ryan Evans. Effective October 9, 2026.

The short version

  • Your calendar, your kids' names and ages, your ZIP code and your settings stay on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive them.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • The app only talks to the internet to get weather (through Apple), download the same daily news file everyone gets, and handle subscriptions (through Apple).

Children

The app is for parents. Kids read the printed paper; they don't use the app or create accounts. Kids' names and ages are entered by a parent and never leave the device. We don't knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Choose which calendars the app can read, or turn calendar access off any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars.
  • Edit or remove kids, ZIP code and never-show words in the app's Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Changes

If how the app handles information changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the change ships.
